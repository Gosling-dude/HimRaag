HimRaag is a music app for Pahadi music: the folk, devotional and modern regional songs of Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh and the wider Himalayan hills.

Listen to Garhwali, Kumaoni, Jaunsari, Himachali and Kinnauri songs in one place. Browse by region, album and artist, and download songs to play when you have no signal.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Stream Garhwali, Kumaoni, Jaunsari, Himachali and Kinnauri songs
• Browse by region, category, album and artist
• Search across songs, artists and albums
• Download songs in the app and listen offline
• Keep music playing in the background, with notification and lock-screen controls
• Save favourites and return to recently played songs
• Choose your favourite regions and get a home screen built around them

SIGN IN OR CONTINUE AS A GUEST
Sign in with Google to keep your favourites, preferences and listening history with your account, or continue as a guest.

FREE, WITH NO ADS
HimRaag is free to use. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Made for everyone who loves the music of the hills.
